Lighting

Philips Hue lights give all the colour. The idea is a gradient strip behind the monitor and another along the wall or ceiling line, set to scenes: pink and blue for evening, neutral white for work.

To confirm

Philips Hue gradient light strips

Philips Hue · desk and wall lighting

Gradient strips blend several colours along their length, and the Hue Sync app can match the colours to what is on screen. The photo shows the Play gradient strip for 32–34" monitors, an older model that is sold out now. The current range is on the Hue strips page.

  • The monitor strip needs a Hue Bridge for screen sync; other strips also work over Bluetooth
  • Needs a power socket near the desk and near the wall run

Gym corner

One corner of the office. Black tatami on the floor over layers that cut noise to the flat below, a pull-up system with different grips, bands for assistance, and a yoga mat for stretching and mobility.

Floor build-up, from the floor up
