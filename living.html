65-inch LG C1 OLED TV, mounted directly on the wall

The TV goes straight on the wall, with no TV unit under it. The C1 supports a VESA wall mount, and a flat or slim tilting mount keeps it close to the wall. Cables would be hidden in the wall or a channel.

  • Screen about 143 × 80 cm (65-inch diagonal), OLED panel
